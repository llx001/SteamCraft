# Soil

SteamCraft adds two special soil blocks that reflect the environmental state of the world: **Rich Soil** and **Poisoned Soil**.

---

## Rich Soil

Rich Soil is fertile ground that rewards players who maintain a clean environment.

### Properties

- **Crop boost:** Crops growing above Rich Soil receive a free, slow growth boost every few seconds — equivalent to a permanent low-level Bonemeal effect.
- **Mob protection:** Hostile mobs **cannot spawn** on Rich Soil, making it excellent for securing base perimeters.
- **Spreading:** In clean air, Rich Soil slowly spreads to adjacent Dirt blocks, expanding your safe and fertile zone.
- **Degradation:** Under severe air pollution, Rich Soil degrades back to ordinary Dirt.

### How to Create Rich Soil

Rich Soil forms naturally through the combination of a clean environment and biological activity. It can also be created by applying **Biomass** (which works like Bonemeal and enriches nearby soil over time). Keep the local air quality high for it to spread on its own.

---

## Poisoned Soil

Poisoned Soil is contaminated ground created by heavy, sustained pollution. It illustrates the direct consequences of environmental damage.

### Properties

- **Player damage:** Walking on Poisoned Soil applies the **Poison** status effect.
- **Crop death:** Plants growing above Poisoned Soil slowly die.
- **Toxin emission:** Poisoned Soil constantly leaches toxins into the surrounding air, making the local air quality worse.
- **Spreading:** When pollution is severe, Poisoned Soil spreads to nearby Dirt blocks.
- **Zombie spawning:** In darkness combined with high air pollution, Poisoned Soil can spawn **Zombies** nearby.

### How to Remove Poisoned Soil

Clean the air — use Air Scrubbers and reduce pollution sources. Once air quality improves and stays clean, Poisoned Soil gradually reverts to ordinary Dirt over time.

---

## Classroom Relevance

The Rich Soil / Poisoned Soil duality is a direct in-game model of real soil health:

- Sustainable farming practices (clean air, organic inputs like Biomass) create fertile, productive soil.
- Industrial pollution and neglect create toxic, unusable land.

Students can observe both outcomes within the same world and experiment with restoring Poisoned Soil back to a healthy state.
