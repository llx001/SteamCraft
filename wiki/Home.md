# SteamCraft Wiki

Welcome to the official wiki for **SteamCraft** — an Erasmus+ educational Minecraft mod that teaches renewable energy, environmental responsibility, and sustainability through hands-on gameplay.

---

## What is SteamCraft?

SteamCraft is a **Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.1** developed as part of the *STEM-Oriented Education in the Virtual World with Customised Minecraft Modifications* project (project reference: 2023-1-HU01-KA220-SCH-000166153). It adds four interconnected environmental systems to Minecraft, each designed to reflect real-world challenges around pollution, energy, food waste, and recycling.

## Quick Facts

- **Minecraft version:** 1.21.1
- **Mod loader:** Fabric
- **Required dependencies:** Fabric API, Patchouli
- **In-game guide:** Use `/steamcraft guide` to receive the *Book of SteamCraft*
- **License:** GNU GPL v3.0
