# Materials & Crafting

SteamCraft adds a layered crafting progression built around three material tiers: **Plastic**, **Steel**, and **Electronics**. This page covers the full processing chain from raw resources to finished components.

---

## Material Tiers at a Glance

| Tier | Source | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| Plastic | Rubber Sap → Rubber → Plastic Sheet | Early tools, Hazmat Gear, insulation |
| Steel | Iron Ingot → Steel Ingot (Blast Furnace) | Advanced tools, machine frames |
| Electronics | Copper → Coils → Wire → Circuit Boards | All powered machines |

---

## Rubber & Plastic Chain

### Step 1 — Collect Rubber Sap

Place a **Tree Tap** against the side of any log. The Tap drills into the log over several cycles and deposits Rubber Sap into its output slot.

- Each log yields **4 Rubber Sap** before the log is consumed and the Tap stops.
- Right-click the Tap to collect the sap, or place a **hopper** underneath to collect automatically.
- No energy is required.

### Step 2 — Smelt Rubber Sap → Rubber

Place Rubber Sap in a furnace (or Blast Furnace for speed).

```
Rubber Sap  →[Furnace]→  Rubber
```

### Step 3 — Smelt Rubber → Plastic Sheet

Smelt Rubber a second time.

```
Rubber  →[Furnace]→  Plastic Sheet
```

Plastic Sheet is the base material for plastic tools, plastic armour, and Hazmat Gear components.

## Plastic Tools

Plastic tools are a fast and cheap early-game option.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Durability | 100 |
| Mining Speed | 8.0 |
| Enchantability | Not enchantable |

Plastic tools can be made into: Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, Sword.

When a plastic tool breaks, put it in the **Recycler** to recover a Plastic Sheet.

### Plastic Armour

| Piece | Armour Points |
|---|---|
| Helmet | 2 |
| Chestplate | 4 |
| Leggings | 6 |
| Boots | 2 |

Repair plastic armour with Plastic Sheet at an anvil. It provides lightweight protection while you work toward Hazmat Gear or Steel equipment.

## Steel Chain

### Step 1 — Smelt Steel Ingot

Steel requires a **Blast Furnace** — a regular furnace will not work.

```
Iron Ingot  →[Blast Furnace]→  Steel Ingot
```

### Step 2 — Cut Plates and Rods (Stonecutter)

Use a **Stonecutter** to process ingots into flat plates and thin rods.

```
Iron Ingot  →[Stonecutter]→  Iron Plate  or  Iron Rod
Copper Ingot  →[Stonecutter]→  Copper Plate  or  Copper Rod
Steel Ingot  →[Stonecutter]→  Steel Plate  or  Steel Rod
```

---

## Steel Tools

Steel tools are the best tier available in SteamCraft.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Durability | 500 |
| Mining Speed | 5.0 (same as diamond) |
| Enchantability | 20 |

Steel tools are crafted by upgrading Iron tools at a **Smithing Table**:

```
Smithing Table:  Iron Tool + Steel Ingot (template)  →  Steel Tool
```

Available upgrades: Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, Sword.

**Tip:** Steel tools last longer than iron and mine as fast as diamond. Recycle broken ones in the **Recycler** to recover a Steel Ingot (70% success rate).

## Electronics Chain

Almost every powered machine requires **Wire** and **Circuit Boards**.

### Wire

```
Copper Rods  →[Crafting]→  Wire
```

### Circuit Board

```
Wire + Copper Coils + Iron Plate  →[Crafting]→  Circuit Board
```

Circuit Boards are used in Batteries, the Portable Scanner, the Environmental Monitor, and other machines.

**Planning tip:** Stock up on Copper early. A single Circuit Board requires multiple Copper Coils, and each Coil requires several Copper Rods. Plan your copper supply before starting large builds.

## Hazmat Gear

Hazmat Gear is crafted from **Rubber** and related materials. It provides full protection against all air quality effects.
